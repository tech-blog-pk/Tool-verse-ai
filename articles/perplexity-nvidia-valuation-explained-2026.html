Perplexity’s Valuation Could Hit $30 Billion: Why Nvidia Wants In

TV
ToolVerse AI
ToolVerse AI Contributor
📅 Aug 27, 2026⏱ 9 min read
🔍

Nvidia is reportedly in talks to invest in AI search startup Perplexity as part of a new funding round that could value the company at more than $30 billion — a jump of over 50% from its $20 billion valuation just a year ago. This guide explains, in easy English, what's driving this surge, why Nvidia is interested, and what it says about where the AI search and agent market is headed.

💡
The headline number: Perplexity's annualized revenue has more than tripled this year alone — from under $250 million in January to over $750 million by August 2026.

What Is Perplexity?

Founded in August 2022 by Aravind Srinivas, Denis Yarats, Johnny Ho, and Andy Konwinski, Perplexity started as an AI-powered search-and-answer engine, offering a conversational alternative to traditional search results. The company has since expanded well beyond search, and its investor list already includes high-profile names like Amazon founder Jeff Bezos and Japan's SoftBank Group.

What's Actually Happening with Nvidia?

  • Nvidia is discussing joining an equity funding round that would value Perplexity above $30 billion
  • This would follow a $23 billion valuation round in January 2026 and a $20 billion round in September 2025
  • Nvidia is already an existing investor in Perplexity, and this would deepen that relationship further
  • Interestingly, Nvidia had previously considered an "acqui-hire" — absorbing Perplexity's technology and team directly — before shifting back toward a straightforward investment instead

What's Fueling the Revenue Growth?

Much of Perplexity's explosive revenue growth traces back to Perplexity Computer, a cloud-based AI agent launched in February 2026 that automates browser and desktop tasks for professional users through usage-based pricing — essentially acting as a digital co-worker that can execute complex, multi-step projects from a single natural-language instruction.

Notably, Perplexity also dropped advertising entirely around the same time it launched this product, yet revenue still climbed roughly 50% month-over-month shortly after — a detail that reportedly caught Nvidia's attention, since strong growth without ad revenue suggests genuine subscription and usage-based demand.

Worth noting: At $750 million in annualized revenue, a $30 billion valuation prices Perplexity at close to 40 times sales — a multiple private investors tolerate far more easily than public stock markets typically would.

Why Nvidia Specifically Cares

  • Deepening ties beyond investment: Perplexity committed in July 2026 to using Nvidia's new Vera CPUs to power its AI-agent workloads, after reportedly finding they significantly outperformed traditional server processors for some tasks
  • Strategic alignment: Perplexity joined Nvidia's Nemotron Coalition in March 2026, backing open AI models as a counterweight to Chinese AI development efforts
  • Nvidia's broader investment pattern: the chipmaker has increasingly invested directly in companies that rely heavily on its hardware and infrastructure, extending its influence beyond just selling chips

Perplexity's Competitive Position

Perplexity hasn't had an entirely smooth competitive run. Once known primarily for its distinctive AI research capabilities, the company has faced pressure as ChatGPT and Claude rolled out their own research modes, leveraging their much larger existing consumer user bases. This context makes Perplexity's pivot toward AI agents — and the resulting revenue surge — particularly significant, since it represents the company carving out a differentiated position in a increasingly crowded market rather than competing head-on for search traffic alone.

What's Next for Perplexity?

CEO Aravind Srinivas has stated the company plans to pursue an IPO in 2028, regardless of how the market receives planned listings from OpenAI and Anthropic. Perplexity has raised more than $1.7 billion to date, and by consistently holding 2028 as its target IPO date, the company says it has been able to focus on building a genuinely high-growth business rather than rushing toward a public listing.

FAQ — Perplexity's Rising Valuation

Has Nvidia's investment in Perplexity been confirmed?

As of this reporting, the investment remains in discussion — neither Nvidia nor Perplexity has publicly confirmed the deal, and there's no guarantee it will ultimately be finalized.

What is Perplexity Computer?

Perplexity Computer is a cloud-based AI agent launched in February 2026 that automates browser and desktop tasks for professional users, acting like a digital co-worker capable of handling complex, multi-step projects from a single instruction.

Why did Perplexity's revenue grow so quickly this year?

The growth is largely attributed to Perplexity Computer's usage-based pricing model, alongside continued expansion of Perplexity's core AI search and agent products, even after the company dropped advertising entirely.

Is a $30 billion valuation considered high for Perplexity?

At roughly 40 times annualized revenue, it's a high multiple by public market standards, though private investors have shown more tolerance for such multiples in fast-growing AI companies during this period.

Does Perplexity compete directly with ChatGPT and Claude?

Yes, to some extent — all three offer AI-powered search and research capabilities, though Perplexity has increasingly differentiated itself through AI agent products like Perplexity Computer rather than competing purely on consumer chatbot features.

Conclusion

Perplexity's potential jump to a $30 billion-plus valuation reflects just how quickly investor confidence in AI search and agent technology has grown throughout 2026, driven by genuinely strong revenue growth rather than speculation alone. Nvidia's interest goes beyond a simple financial bet — it reflects a broader strategy of deepening ties with companies that rely heavily on its hardware and infrastructure.

Whether or not this specific funding round closes at the reported terms, Perplexity's trajectory — from a $1 billion valuation to over $30 billion in roughly two years — is a clear signal of how fast capital continues flowing into the AI search and agent space, and how quickly market leaders in this category can shift. 🔍
