Best AI Chrome Extensions in 2026: Top 10 Must-Have Tools

TV
ToolVerse AI
ToolVerse AI Contributor
📅 Jul 8, 2026⏱ 11 min read
🔌

Your Chrome browser is where you spend most of your online time — reading, writing, researching, and working. AI Chrome extensions bring powerful AI capabilities directly into that space, without requiring you to open a separate app or copy-paste content back and forth. In 2026, the best AI browser extensions can summarize web pages, improve your writing, answer questions about any content you are reading, and save research in organized notes — all from inside Chrome.

This guide covers the top 10 AI Chrome extensions in 2026, what each one actually does, who it is best for, and what you can expect from the free and paid plans.

💡
Before you install: Only install Chrome extensions from the official Chrome Web Store. Check the permission requests carefully — a writing assistant does not need access to your camera or microphone. If an extension requests permissions that do not match its stated purpose, do not install it.

What Are AI Chrome Extensions?

AI Chrome extensions are small software add-ons that install directly into your Chrome browser and add AI-powered features to your everyday browsing. Unlike standalone AI apps, they work in context — they can read the page you are on, assist with text you are typing in any website's text field, or provide information without leaving your current tab.

1. Grammarly — Best for Writing Across the Web

Grammarly is the most widely used AI writing assistant Chrome extension in the world. It works inside Gmail, Google Docs, LinkedIn, Twitter, and virtually every other text field across the web. The free plan covers grammar, spelling, and basic tone corrections. The paid plan adds advanced style suggestions, a plagiarism checker, and full-sentence rewrites.

Real-World Example

You are writing an important email in Gmail. Grammarly highlights a sentence that is grammatically awkward, suggests a cleaner version, and flags that your tone might come across as too casual for a professional context — all without you leaving the email compose window.

  • ✅ Works in almost every text field across every website
  • ✅ Real-time grammar, spelling, and tone corrections
  • ✅ Free plan covers the most important features
  • ❌ Advanced suggestions and plagiarism check require paid plan
  • ❌ Can sometimes over-correct natural writing style

Best for: Writers, students, professionals, and anyone who writes emails or documents in English.

2. Merlin AI — Best All-Round AI Assistant Extension

Merlin AI is one of the most feature-rich free AI Chrome extensions available in 2026. It gives you access to multiple AI models (including GPT-4, Claude, and Gemini) directly in Chrome, lets you summarize any web page or YouTube video, and provides a sidebar AI chat that works on any website. The free plan includes a generous number of daily queries.

  • ✅ Access to multiple AI models in one extension
  • ✅ Summarizes web pages and YouTube videos instantly
  • ✅ Works as a sidebar chat on any website
  • ✅ Free plan with daily query allowance
  • ❌ Free queries run out quickly if you use it heavily
  • ❌ Interface can feel cluttered with so many features

Best for: Researchers, students, and power users who want access to multiple AI models in one place.

3. Monica AI — Best for Comprehensive AI Assistance

Monica AI is a comprehensive AI assistant extension that works across search engines, web pages, and documents. It can summarize content, translate pages, explain selected text, generate images, and chat with any document you have open. Monica uses GPT-4 and Claude models and offers a meaningful free tier with daily usage credits.

  • ✅ Works across search results, web pages, and PDFs
  • ✅ Translates web pages into your language instantly
  • ✅ Explain, summarize, or rewrite any selected text
  • ✅ Image generation built in
  • ❌ Premium models and higher usage require paid plan
  • ❌ Can feel overwhelming for users who only need one or two features

Best for: Multilingual users, researchers, and anyone who wants a comprehensive AI assistant in Chrome.

4. ChatGPT for Chrome (by OpenAI) — Best for Direct GPT Access

OpenAI's official Chrome extension brings ChatGPT directly into your browser as a quick-access panel. You can open it with a keyboard shortcut, ask questions, get help with whatever you are reading, or draft text without leaving your current tab. It uses your existing ChatGPT account, so your conversations and custom instructions carry over.

  • ✅ Official OpenAI extension — trusted and reliable
  • ✅ Opens with keyboard shortcut for quick access
  • ✅ Uses your existing ChatGPT account and settings
  • ✅ Free with a ChatGPT account
  • ❌ GPT-4o access requires a ChatGPT Plus subscription
  • ❌ Less feature-rich than third-party extensions like Merlin or Monica

Best for: Existing ChatGPT users who want quick browser access without opening a new tab.

5. Sider AI — Best for Reading and Research Workflows

Sider AI is a sidebar extension that adds a persistent AI panel on the right side of your browser. It can summarize the page you are reading, answer questions about the content, translate selected text, and help you write responses — all without leaving the page. It supports multiple AI models including GPT-4 and Claude.

  • ✅ Persistent sidebar — always accessible without switching tabs
  • ✅ Summarizes any web page in seconds
  • ✅ Answers questions specifically about the page you are reading
  • ✅ Generous free plan with daily usage
  • ❌ Sidebar takes up screen space on smaller displays
  • ❌ Advanced AI models require paid subscription

Best for: Researchers, students, and anyone who reads a lot of content online and wants AI assistance in context.

6. Compose AI — Best for Autocomplete and Writing Speed

Compose AI focuses on one specific and very useful feature: AI-powered autocomplete for any text field on any website. It learns your writing style and suggests completions as you type — similar to how Gmail's Smart Compose works, but available across every website. The free plan is genuinely useful for everyday writing acceleration.

  • ✅ AI autocomplete in any text field across the web
  • ✅ Learns your writing patterns over time
  • ✅ Saves time on repetitive writing tasks like emails
  • ✅ Free plan available
  • ❌ More limited than full AI assistants for research or summarization
  • ❌ Works best for routine, repetitive writing rather than creative tasks

Best for: Professionals and students who write many similar emails or messages and want to speed up the process.

7. Wiseone — Best for Understanding Complex Articles

Wiseone is designed specifically for reading comprehension. It adds an AI layer to any article you are reading — you can hover over unfamiliar terms for instant definitions, ask questions about the content, get a quick summary, and explore related topics. It is particularly useful for academic papers, news articles, and technical content.

  • ✅ Hover to get instant definitions and explanations of any term
  • ✅ Summarizes complex articles into key points
  • ✅ Ask questions about the content you are reading
  • ✅ Great for reading academic papers and technical content
  • ❌ Free plan has daily usage limits
  • ❌ Works best on article and document pages — less useful for general browsing

Best for: Students, researchers, and anyone reading complex technical or academic content online.

8. Perplexity AI Extension — Best for Search Replacement

The Perplexity AI Chrome extension lets you get AI-powered, cited answers from any search bar — replacing or supplementing traditional Google searches with direct, sourced answers. You can also highlight any text on a webpage and ask Perplexity a question about it instantly.

  • ✅ AI-powered search with cited sources from any browser search bar
  • ✅ Highlight and ask — instantly query any selected text
  • ✅ Free to use with a Perplexity account
  • ❌ Requires changing your default search behavior
  • ❌ Less useful for tasks other than search and research

Best for: Researchers and students who want sourced AI answers integrated into their search workflow.

9. LINER — Best for Highlighting and Saving Research

LINER is a web highlighter and AI research tool combined. You can highlight text on any webpage or PDF, save it to your LINER library, and then use AI to summarize, ask questions about, or organize your saved highlights. It is particularly useful for students and researchers who collect information from multiple sources.

  • ✅ Highlight and save content from any webpage or PDF
  • ✅ AI summarizes and answers questions about your highlights
  • ✅ Organizes research into collections by topic
  • ✅ Free plan with basic highlighting features
  • ❌ AI features require the premium plan for full access
  • ❌ Library can become disorganized if you do not tag highlights consistently

Best for: Students and researchers who gather information from multiple sources and need a structured way to organize it.

10. Glasp — Best for Social Learning and Knowledge Sharing

Glasp combines web highlighting with a social learning platform. You highlight content, add notes, and your highlights are optionally shared with a community of learners. You can also see what other people have highlighted from the same article — which often surfaces insights you might have missed. It integrates with Notion and other note-taking tools.

  • ✅ Highlight and annotate any webpage or PDF
  • ✅ See what others have highlighted from the same article
  • ✅ Integrates with Notion, Readwise, and other tools
  • ✅ Completely free
  • ❌ Social features require sharing your highlights publicly by default
  • ❌ Less AI-powered than other tools on this list

Best for: Students and knowledge workers who want to learn from a community and build a personal knowledge base.

How to Install AI Chrome Extensions

  • Open Chrome and go to the Chrome Web Store (chrome.google.com/webstore)
  • Search for the extension by name
  • Click "Add to Chrome" on the official extension page
  • Review the permissions requested — only grant what is necessary
  • The extension icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar once installed
  • Pin frequently used extensions to your toolbar for quick access

Privacy and Security Tips

  • Read permission requests carefully: A writing extension should not need access to your camera, contacts, or location
  • Check the developer: Extensions from well-known companies (OpenAI, Grammarly) are safer than unknown developers
  • Review reviews and install numbers: Widely installed extensions with recent positive reviews are generally safer
  • Limit extensions to what you use: Every extension you install has access to your browsing data — install only what you actually use
  • Do not enter sensitive data: Avoid typing passwords, financial information, or confidential business data into AI extension interfaces

FAQ — AI Chrome Extensions

Are AI Chrome extensions safe to use?

Extensions from reputable developers published in the official Chrome Web Store are generally safe. The main risk is privacy — extensions can read content from web pages you visit. Stick to well-known extensions with large install bases and read permission requests before installing anything.

Do AI Chrome extensions slow down my browser?

Installing too many extensions can slow Chrome down. Most AI extensions are lightweight, but having 10 or more active extensions simultaneously can impact browser performance. Keep only the extensions you regularly use and disable or remove the rest.

Are these AI Chrome extensions free?

All 10 extensions in this list have free plans. Some like Grammarly and Glasp are fully functional for free. Others like Merlin, Monica, and Sider have daily usage limits on the free plan and charge for unlimited or premium model access.

Which AI Chrome extension should a beginner install first?

Start with Grammarly — it is the most universally useful, works everywhere, and provides immediate value without requiring any learning curve. If you also want AI summarization and Q&A capabilities, add Sider AI or Merlin as a second extension.

Can I use multiple AI Chrome extensions at the same time?

Yes — multiple extensions can be active simultaneously. However, some extensions may conflict if they both try to add UI elements to the same page. If you experience visual glitches or slow loading on certain pages, try disabling extensions one at a time to identify the conflict.

Conclusion

The best AI Chrome extensions in 2026 — Grammarly, Merlin AI, Monica AI, ChatGPT for Chrome, Sider AI, Compose AI, Wiseone, Perplexity, LINER, and Glasp — turn your browser into a powerful AI-assisted productivity environment. Most of them are free to start, and the right combination depends entirely on how you spend your time online.

Start with one or two extensions that match your primary use case, get comfortable with them, and add others selectively. A well-chosen set of AI browser extensions can save you significant time every day — without requiring you to leave the web pages you are already working on. 🔌
