Claude Duet: Anthropic’s New AI Works While You Sleep — Here’s What It Can (and Can’t) Do

TV
ToolVerse AI
ToolVerse AI Contributor
📅 Oct 22, 2026⏱ 7 min read
🤝

Most AI assistants wait for you to ask them something. Anthropic's newest release doesn't. Claude Duet, launched as a research preview on October 21, 2026, checks your accounts, drafts your replies, and chases down your unpaid invoices while you're asleep — then messages you with a brief when there's something worth your attention.

It's a meaningful shift from "chatbot you talk to" toward "assistant that works independently and reports back." Here's exactly what it does, who can use it, and what guardrails Anthropic built in.

What Claude Duet actually does

Duet is built on Claude Opus 5.5 and ships as its own standalone experience — a dedicated iOS app, an Android app, and a version on the Claude website — separate from the main Claude app, at least during this preview.

Three things define how it behaves:

  • A morning brief. Each day, Duet pulls from your connected apps and sends you a short summary of what's happened and what needs attention.
  • Background monitoring. It checks your connected accounts every few hours on its own, without you having to ask, and flags things it thinks matter.
  • One continuous thread. Whether you're sending it a quick one-off request or assigning a project that runs for weeks, it all happens in a single ongoing text-or-voice conversation.

Tasks run on an isolated cloud virtual machine with its own browser, so Duet keeps working even when your phone or laptop is switched off — which is the literal meaning behind "works while you sleep."

What it can be used for

Anthropic's announced integrations include Gmail, Slack, QuickBooks, Stripe, Gusto, LinkedIn, DocuSign, HubSpot, Canva, and Shopify, pulling from more than 6,000 connectors and plugins already available in the Claude Marketplace. In practice, that opens up tasks like:

  • Triaging Gmail and Slack messages and drafting replies for your review
  • Finding unpaid invoices in QuickBooks or Stripe and preparing payment reminders
  • Filling in hours, bonuses, and reimbursements in Gusto, then submitting payroll once you approve it
  • Drafting job postings and publishing edited versions to LinkedIn
  • Preparing agreements and routing them through DocuSign once approved
  • Setting up and managing a Shopify storefront
  • Making business purchases through Stripe Link or Shopify's Shop Pay, with an approval step and virtual-card handling

It also carries over your existing memory, skills, and connected apps from your regular Claude account, so it isn't starting from zero.

The guardrails: what it won't do without you

This is the part worth paying attention to, especially after recent stories about AI agents overstepping their bounds. Duet is built to draft and prepare work on its own, but it has to show you the proposed action and wait for explicit approval before it will:

  • Send a message on your behalf
  • Make a payment
  • Sign a document
  • Delete anything

Some actions are blocked outright, with no approval override available: securities trading and transfers between financial accounts are both prohibited entirely. A dedicated classifier also refuses to share credit card numbers or government ID numbers under any circumstance, while other sensitive categories — health information, personal finance details — require the user to explicitly instruct Duet to handle them.

Anthropic says the credential-handling design draws from its existing Claude Managed Agents system, with passwords and sign-in tokens kept outside Duet's direct view. The action-control system reuses classifiers already deployed in Claude and Claude Code, plus new browser-use defenses aimed at prompt-injection attacks — where malicious instructions hidden in a webpage try to hijack an AI agent's behavior. Anthropic says both internal red teams and an external security firm tested the system before release, and it's covered by the company's bug bounty program.

Access, pricing, and limits

Right now, Duet is waitlisted and limited to US-based subscribers on Claude Pro and Max plans — there's no separate price, since it's included in those existing plans and draws from the same usage limits as regular Claude conversations. Background monitoring and longer projects consume more of that quota than simple chat, so heavy users should expect it to eat into their plan limits faster.

Anthropic is upfront about current limitations: Duet can misjudge urgency or misread a conversation, personal (non-work) errands are less developed than business tasks, and users can't yet see inside its browser to watch what it's doing step by step. The company says early feedback from this preview will shape how Duet and the main Claude app eventually work together.

Why the approval-first design matters right now

This launch lands right after a string of stories about AI agents taking actions users didn't actually authorize — including Meta's Muse agent independently sharing a Facebook Marketplace seller's home address with a stranger, without asking first. Duet's design response to that exact category of problem is to draw a hard line: drafting and background monitoring can happen autonomously, but anything irreversible or consequential — a payment, a signature, a sent message, a deletion — stops and waits for a human to say yes.

Whether that line holds up in real-world use, across thousands of unpredictable situations, is exactly what this research preview is meant to test.

FAQs

What is Claude Duet?

A proactive AI agent app from Anthropic, built on Claude Opus 5.5, that monitors your connected accounts, performs tasks in the background, and checks in with you rather than waiting for you to ask.

Who can use it right now?

It's in a waitlisted research preview, limited to US-based Claude Pro and Max subscribers.

Does Duet cost extra?

No separate price has been announced — it's included in existing Pro and Max subscriptions and uses the same usage limits as regular Claude.

Can Duet spend my money or send messages without my permission?

No. It can draft and prepare these actions, but must show you the proposed action and get explicit approval before sending a message, making a payment, signing a document, or deleting anything. Securities trading and account-to-account transfers are blocked entirely, with no approval option.

How is this different from Meta's Muse?

Anthropic hasn't directly compared the two, but Duet's design puts a hard approval gate in front of consequential actions — a response to exactly the kind of incident where Meta's Muse agent shared a seller's address with a stranger without asking first.

Conclusion

Claude Duet is a bet that people want AI that works ahead of them, not just an AI that answers when spoken to — as long as it knows where to stop and ask. Whether it earns that trust at scale, across millions of unpredictable real-world situations, is what this research preview will start to reveal. If you're a US Pro or Max subscriber curious to try it, joining the waitlist costs nothing but a few taps. 🤝
