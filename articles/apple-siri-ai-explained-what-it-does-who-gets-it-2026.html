Apple’s New Siri AI Explained: What It Can Do, Which iPhones Get It, and How to Turn It On

TV
ToolVerse AI
ToolVerse AI Contributor
📅 Nov 23, 2026⏱ 9 min read
🎙️

If you own an iPhone, you've probably noticed that Siri has been getting a big upgrade in iOS 27. Apple calls it Siri AI, and in November 2026 Apple began rolling out new capabilities across its own apps and opened the door for third-party apps. Here's a plain-English guide to what it is, what it can do, which devices get it, what the limits are, and how to switch it on.

What is Siri AI?

Siri AI is Apple's overhaul of Siri. Instead of only answering simple commands, it's designed to hold a conversation, understand context, and take actions across your apps. According to MacRumors and Apple's own announcements, it can:

  • Use your personal context: find things in your emails, texts, files, photos, and notes.
  • Use broad world knowledge and look up current information on the web.
  • Understand your screen: answer questions about what you're looking at.
  • Take actions in apps: for example, drafting an email or editing and sharing photos.
  • Hold multi-turn conversations, through a dedicated Siri app with cross-device sync, and help with writing and proofreading anywhere you type.

Old Siri vs Siri AI

Old Siri Siri AI
Mostly one command at a time Multi-turn conversations that remember what you just said
Limited knowledge, often “here’s what I found on the web” Broader world knowledge and current information
Little understanding of your own data Can search your emails, messages, files, photos and notes (personal context)
Doesn’t know what’s on your screen Can answer questions about what’s on your screen
Basic phone commands Can take actions inside apps, including multi-step ones

What's new in November 2026

On November 3, Apple announced that it had started rolling out new Siri AI capabilities in eight of its own apps: Calendar, Files, Mail, Maps, Messages, Photos, Safari, and Weather. MacRumors also reported examples from the iOS 27.3 developer release:

  • Mail: unsubscribe from mailing lists and undo recently sent messages.
  • Safari: clear browsing history, manage tabs and windows, save articles to the Reading List, and close duplicate tabs.
  • Weather: add locations to your list and control weather notifications.
  • Files: organize content and delete files you haven't opened for a set period.

You can also ask Siri AI what each app can do, which is a handy way to discover commands.

Apple also opened a Developer Preview so any developer can connect their app to Siri AI through "App Intents." That could eventually let you say things like "check my delivery," "book a table," or "place my usual order" in third-party apps. But there's a catch: those integrations can't ship to regular users yet. Apple's announcement says first customer shipments are expected "in a few months" and App Store submissions open in the spring, and those are estimates, not promises.

Which devices can use it?

According to Apple's support page, Siri AI needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence and the iOS 27 generation of software:

Device Needs
iPhone iOS 27 on iPhone 15 Pro and later, iPhone 16 and later (including the Air models)
iPad iPadOS 27 on iPad mini (A17 Pro and newer) and iPad with M1 or later
Mac macOS 27 on Mac with M1 or later
Apple Watch watchOS 27 on Series 9 or later, Ultra 2 or later, SE 3, with a paired Siri AI-enabled iPhone nearby
Apple Vision Pro visionOS 27 on any Apple Vision Pro

Apple's page notes that features vary by region, language, hardware, and software version. Some extras, like customizable "expressive" voices, need newer hardware and are English-only.

Which languages and countries?

Apple's support page lists the supported languages as English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish, with Traditional Chinese, Norwegian, Turkish, and Vietnamese planned by the end of the year. Your device language and Siri language must match a supported language, and your Apple Account must be in an eligible region.

  • EU: not available on iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch for now. Apple says it's working with the EU on a path forward. It's available on Mac and Vision Pro if your Apple Account region is in the EU.
  • Mainland China: doesn't work if your Apple Account region is in mainland China.
  • Age: not available to users under 13.

What about Pakistan and Urdu? Urdu isn't on the supported language list. An earlier MacRumors guide listed English availability for specific countries (the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland, India, New Zealand and South Africa) and did not name Pakistan, but Apple's current page says only that your account must be in an "eligible region" without listing every country. So we can't confirm Pakistan availability. The quickest way to find out is to check Settings → Siri on your iPhone, or Apple's "How to get Siri AI" support page for your region.

How to turn on Siri AI (beta)

  1. Update your device to the latest supported iOS 27 version.
  2. Open Settings → Siri. If Siri is off, turn it on first.
  3. Tap Try Siri AI (Beta).
  4. Follow the on-screen steps: choose a voice, complete voice enrollment, and review privacy settings.
  5. Wait for it to download. During busy periods, Apple says you may be placed on a waitlist.

If you don't see the option, it likely means your device, language, or account region isn't supported yet.

What about privacy?

MacRumors reports that Siri AI processes requests on-device and on Apple's Private Cloud Compute servers, and says the data is not accessible to Apple or third parties. It also reports that Apple's foundation models were developed with Google, using technology behind the Gemini models, and that Apple is expanding Private Cloud Compute to run on Google Cloud with NVIDIA hardware. Those are descriptions from press coverage of Apple's announcements; read Apple's privacy settings screen during setup for the details that apply to you. A good habit with any assistant that can read your messages and files is to review what it's allowed to access.

Limits to know about

  • It's labeled beta. Expect rough edges and changes.
  • Daily usage limits. Apple says server-side features, including Siri AI, have daily limits that vary by feature, request complexity, and demand, and that expanded access will be available for a fee in the future.
  • Third-party app actions aren't live yet for regular users.
  • Check actions before confirming. When an assistant can delete files or send messages, always review what it's about to do.

How is it different from Google's AI agent?

Siri AI is built into Apple devices and focuses on your personal data and in-app actions. Google's Gemini "Tasks" runs multi-step jobs in the background and is aimed at planning, research, and Google Workspace work. We cover that one in our guide to Gemini Tasks, Google's free AI agent.

FAQs

Is Siri AI free?

Apple's support page doesn't describe a price for the beta, but says server-side features have daily usage limits and that expanded access will be available for a fee in the future.

Will it work on my older iPhone?

Per Apple's list, you need an iPhone 15 Pro or later (or an iPhone 16 or later). Older models aren't listed.

Can I use it in Urdu?

Urdu isn't on Apple's supported language list at the time of writing. You could use a supported language, if your region is eligible, but check Apple's page.

Why can't I see "Try Siri AI" in Settings?

Common reasons: your device isn't supported, your software isn't updated, your device and Siri languages don't match a supported language, or your Apple Account is in a region that isn't eligible yet.

Does Siri AI send my data to third parties?

Reports describe on-device and Private Cloud Compute processing with strong privacy claims. Read the privacy screen during setup and Apple's documentation to understand exactly what applies.

Conclusion

Siri AI is Apple's biggest assistant upgrade in years: more conversational, aware of your own information, and able to act inside apps. It's still in beta, availability depends on your device, language, and region, and many third-party features are months away. If your iPhone is supported, it's worth trying carefully; if not, watch for expansion, and in the meantime, the same safety habit applies to every AI helper: check before you confirm. 🎙️
