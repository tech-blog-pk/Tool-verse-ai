How to Get a Free Year of Google Gemini Pro as a Student

TV
ToolVerse AI
ToolVerse AI Contributor
📅 Aug 25, 2026⏱ 9 min read
🎓

Starting August 19, 2026, Google relaunched its student AI offer for the new academic year — giving eligible college students a full year of a premium Gemini plan completely free, alongside a redesigned student hub packed with study tools. This guide explains, in easy English, exactly who qualifies, what you get, and how to claim it before the deadline.

💡
Important: A valid payment method is required at signup, and the plan automatically converts to a paid subscription after 12 months unless you cancel. Set a calendar reminder before you claim it.

What's Being Offered?

  • US college students: one free year of Google AI Pro (normally $19.99/month, worth about $240)
  • Students in 140+ other countries: one free year of Google AI Plus (normally around $4.99/month, pricing varies by region)
  • Bundle option: Google AI Pro plus YouTube Premium for $8.99/month in the US — up to 70% off the regular combined price

What's Included in Google AI Pro (US Students)

  • 4x higher usage limits in Gemini compared to the free tier
  • Access to Gemini Spark, an AI agent feature
  • Gemini integrated directly into Gmail, Google Docs, and other Google apps
  • 5TB of cloud storage
  • Google Health Premium included as well

What's Included in Google AI Plus (International Students)

  • 2x higher usage limits compared to the free tier
  • Access to Gemini Omni
  • 400GB of cloud storage

Who's Eligible?

  • Eligible college/university students, verified through a service called SheerID
  • You'll need proof of enrollment, like a student ID or current enrollment transcript, in case manual verification is triggered
  • Must be claimed on a personal Google account — Google Workspace for Education accounts do not qualify
  • Offer must be redeemed by December 31, 2026

How to Claim the Offer

  1. Go to gemini.google.com/students
  2. Sign in with your personal Google account
  3. Verify your student status through SheerID (have your student ID or enrollment proof ready)
  4. Enter a valid payment method (required, even though the first year is free)
  5. Confirm your plan and start using the student hub
⚡
Don't forget: Set a personal reminder a few weeks before your 12-month free period ends. The subscription auto-converts to paid unless you actively cancel — US students will be charged $19.99/month, and international students $4.99/month (or local equivalent) after the trial.

New Student Hub and Study Tools

Alongside the free subscription, Google has built a dedicated student hub inside the Gemini app, bringing together several new learning-focused features:

  • Study notebooks: break complex topics into manageable, bite-sized lessons, now supporting graphs and images
  • Flashcards and practice quizzes: generated automatically from your course material
  • Interactive 3D visualizations: help illustrate difficult concepts, especially in science and math
  • Deep Research via Gemini Live: hands-free, conversational research and study sessions
  • Calendar integration: Gemini can add test dates and deadlines to your Google Calendar based on your syllabus

Google Search Is Getting Study Tools Too

Separately from Gemini, Google Search now offers free practice quizzes through AI Overviews and AI Mode, covering subjects like science, math, humanities, and foreign languages — plus prep for major standardized tests including the SAT, ACT, GRE, MCAT, and LSAT, available globally in English at no cost.

Real-World Example: Using the Offer This Semester

Imagine a computer science student juggling multiple courses. After claiming Google AI Pro, they use the student hub to build a study notebook for their hardest class, automatically generating flashcards from lecture notes and letting Gemini scan the syllabus to add every exam date directly to their Google Calendar — cutting down significantly on manual planning at the start of the semester.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting to cancel before the free year ends: the subscription auto-renews as paid unless canceled in advance
  • Using a school Workspace account: Google Workspace for Education accounts don't qualify — use a personal account instead
  • Not having verification documents ready: keep a photo of your student ID or enrollment proof handy in case SheerID requires manual review
  • Missing the deadline: the offer must be claimed by December 31, 2026

FAQ — Free Google Gemini for Students

Do I need to pay anything upfront to claim this offer?

No payment is charged upfront, but a valid payment method is required at signup since the subscription automatically converts to paid after the 12-month free period unless canceled.

Can high school students claim this offer?

This offer is specifically for eligible college and university students verified through SheerID; high school students are not included in this particular promotion.

What happens if I don't cancel before the free year ends?

Your subscription will automatically convert to a paid plan — $19.99/month for US students on Google AI Pro, or $4.99/month (or local equivalent) for international students on Google AI Plus.

Is this the same as the earlier 2025 Google student offer?

No, this is a relaunched offer for the 2026-2027 academic year, with some differences in terms and regional availability compared to the previous promotion that ended in April 2026.

Do I need a Google Workspace for Education account to qualify?

No — the offer specifically requires a personal Google account; Google Workspace for Education accounts are not eligible for this promotion.

Conclusion

Google's relaunched student offer gives eligible college students genuinely useful, premium AI tools at no cost for a full year — from significantly higher usage limits to a purpose-built study hub with notebooks, quizzes, and calendar integration. Combined with the free practice quizzes now available through Google Search, it's a solid back-to-school resource worth claiming if you qualify.

Just remember: set a reminder before the free year ends, keep your verification documents handy, and use a personal Google account when signing up. Claim it, explore the student hub, and put those study tools to work this semester. 🎓
