How to Build a Free Website in 2026

TV
ToolVerse AI
ToolVerse AI Contributor
📅 Jun 22, 2026⏱ 6 min read
🌐

Building a website used to cost hundreds of dollars and require a professional developer. In 2026, that has completely changed. You can build and publish a professional-looking website for free — no coding experience needed. This guide walks you through exactly how to do it, step by step.

Why Build a Website in 2026?

Whether you are a student, freelancer, small business owner, or just someone with something to share — having your own website gives you a professional online presence that no social media profile can replace. You own it completely. No algorithm decides who sees it.

  • Showcase your portfolio or resume to employers
  • Sell products or services without paying platform fees
  • Share your ideas, blog, or creative work with the world
  • Build credibility for your personal brand or business

What You Need Before You Start

Nothing expensive. Here is the complete list of what you need:

  • A computer or smartphone with internet access
  • A free Google or GitHub account
  • A clear idea of what your website is about
  • About 2 to 3 hours of your time for the first version
💡
Good news: You do not need to know HTML, CSS, or any programming language to build a great website in 2026. The tools available today handle all of that for you automatically.

Option 1 — GitHub Pages (Best for Developers and Bloggers)

GitHub Pages is completely free and lets you host any website directly from your GitHub account. There are no ads, no bandwidth limits, and your site gets a clean URL like yourname.github.io. This is exactly how ToolVerse AI is hosted.

How to Get Started with GitHub Pages

  • Create a free account at github.com
  • Create a new repository — make it Public
  • Upload your file named index.html
  • Go to Settings → Pages → select the main branch
  • Your site goes live in 2 to 3 minutes

GitHub Pages works best if you already have an HTML file ready or want to learn basic web development. If you want something visual and drag-and-drop, the next options will suit you better.

Option 2 — Framer (Best for Beautiful Design)

Framer is one of the most impressive free website builders available in 2026. It uses AI to generate entire website layouts from a simple text description. You type what you want — Framer builds it. The free plan gives you a published website with a Framer subdomain.

  • AI generates your entire layout from a text description
  • Drag and drop editor — fully visual, zero coding
  • Mobile responsive by default — looks great on all devices
  • Free plan includes hosting and an SSL security certificate

Option 3 — Carrd (Best for Simple One-Page Sites)

Carrd is perfect for portfolios, landing pages, and personal websites. It is incredibly simple — pick a template, customize it, and publish. The free plan gives you up to three websites with a carrd.co subdomain.

  • Dozens of clean, professional free templates to choose from
  • Takes less than 30 minutes to publish your first site
  • Looks great on mobile without any extra work
  • Free plan supports up to 3 separate websites

Option 4 — Notion + Super (Best for Writers)

If you already use Notion for notes, you can turn any Notion page into a real website using Super.so. Write your content in Notion like you normally would — Super converts it into a fast, clean website automatically.

  • No design skills needed — Notion is your editor
  • Your website updates instantly when you edit Notion
  • Clean, readable design that loads very fast
  • Super.so has a free trial to help you get started
⚠️
Important: Every free plan comes with a subdomain like yourname.carrd.co or yourname.github.io. If you want a custom domain like yourname.com, you will need to buy one — usually around $10 to $15 per year. But for getting started, the free subdomain works perfectly well.

Choosing the Right Platform for You

Here is a simple way to decide which platform matches your situation:

  • You can write basic HTML and want full control → GitHub Pages
  • You want a beautiful design with no coding at all → Framer
  • You need a simple one-page site built fast → Carrd
  • You write a lot and want maximum simplicity → Notion + Super

Step-by-Step: Your First Website in 3 Hours

Here is a realistic plan to go from zero to published today:

  • Hour 1: Decide your website purpose and pick a platform from this list
  • Hour 2: Choose a template, add your content — name, bio, links, and a photo
  • Hour 3: Review everything on your phone, fix any issues, and hit publish

That is it. Your website is live. You can keep improving it over time — but getting something published is always better than waiting for it to be perfect.

What to Put on Your First Website

Keep it simple for your first version. You do not need dozens of pages. Start with these four essentials:

  • Home page: Who you are and what your site is about
  • About section: Your background, skills, or personal story
  • Work or projects: Examples of what you have built or created
  • Contact: How people can reach you — your email or social links

Conclusion

Building a free website in 2026 has never been more accessible. You have powerful platforms available at zero cost — GitHub Pages for developers, Framer for designers, Carrd for simplicity, and Notion for writers. Pick the one that fits your situation, spend a few hours on it, and publish something real.

The best website is the one that actually exists. Start simple, publish fast, and improve it over time. Your online presence is one of the most valuable things you can build — and in 2026, it costs you nothing but a few hours of your time. 🌐
